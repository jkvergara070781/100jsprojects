How many half steps (semitones) are in a perfect fifth?

A perfect fifth spans 7 semitones, as in C to G. This interval is considered highly consonant and forms the foundation of many chords, including the basic major and minor triads.

Which mode has the same notes as a natural minor scale but starts on the 6th degree of a major scale?

The Aeolian mode is identical to the natural minor scale and begins on the sixth degree of its relative major scale. For example, A Aeolian uses the exact same notes as C major, just starting and centering on A instead.

What type of chord is built by stacking a major third and then a minor third on top of a root note?

A major triad is formed by stacking a major third followed by a minor third above the root, such as C-E-G. This combination gives the chord its bright, stable, and consonant sound, which is why it's the most common chord type in Western music.
